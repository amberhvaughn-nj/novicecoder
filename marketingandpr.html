    • Marketing and PR

    I initially went to an out-of-state university in Connecticut named University of Bridgeport to study Fashion Merchandising. When that didn't work out, I spent a year working in retail then started over at New Jersey City University in Spring 2011.

    While selecting my classes towards the end of my freshman year, I added a Marketing course to my schedule. As the professor began teaching the class, I was hooked onto the lessons she taught and how she explained everything. When I spoke to her, she explained that I wasn't supposed to be taking the class because there was a prerequisite that I didn't have. She she also told me the type of learner I am: visual learner (I learn better by seeing and doing instead of just listening).

    I declared my major as Marketing in the beginning of my sophomore year, and she took me under her wing. She began setting me up with a roadmap of classes to take with professsors who taught the same way as her - with a goal of me graduating in Spring of 2017.

    When I became a junior, I enrolled in a Public Relations course that further solidified my interest in the field. The professor's teaching style resonated with my visual learning preferences, and I found myself genuinely excited about the subject matter. I built a rapport with her and was able to create a IMC plan for a local non-profit organization. Even though my plan wasn't chosen to be implemented, it something that could be used to create a portfolio.

    During this time, I was writing in the school newspaper and managing the social media accounts, and working as an intern for a local non-profit organization. During my internship, I was able to write a press release and sent it to local media outlets. I was able to get a few of the media outlets to publish my press release, which was a great accomplishment for me as a novice in the field. I also made an event flyer that was used in a email campaign. Both of these items were included in the portfolio.

    In my senior year, I enrolled in three different classes that was taught by the same professor who taught the Public Relations course. I was able to learn more about marketing and public relations materials used in the marketing field, and wrote a senior campstone around social media and how it's used impact consumer behavior.
